Admissions — the sequence traps

GRE is needed for BOTH TSU steps, never for PVAMU. Scores die at 5 years — a GRE taken for MUPEP entry can expire before PhD entry; plan on a second sitting

The GPA gate that matters most is the MASTER'S GPA (PhD wants 3.2–3.3 in master's coursework) — protect it at PVAMU and at TSU

Letters: 3 at every step; the PhD wants at least two from faculty — build faculty relationships during the master's programs

The PVAMU degree transfers 0 hrs into the MUPEP (verified) — its value is the credential and the GPA, not hours

Two official conflicts to settle by email before applying: PVAMU's real master's deadlines; TSU's real PhD GPA floor (3.2 vs 3.3)

Bachelor's step — concentration call (Nov 24, 2026)

Admission fact: none of the three programs requires or prefers a bachelor's field — PVAMU takes any bachelor's, MUPEP takes any field, the PhD judges the master's. The concentration buys background, not admission

Best fit: Real Estate Construction and Portfolio Development — construction management + residential and commercial portfolio development; the development side of community development, and it feeds the MUPEP's Community Development and Housing specialization

Not the fit: Real Estate Marketing Management — the sell/lease side (agent and brokerage work); a TREC license is the lever for that, not a concentration

Same-result option: concentrations do not print on the APUS diploma — staying General and taking the same 3 courses (BUSN201, BUSN301, BUSN302, BUSN425 — pick 3) inside the 9-hr slot gives the same knowledge at the same cost

Different major: NO — at 84/120 hrs a switch risks credits and the timeline (SkillBridge Jun 5, 2027; ETS Dec 2, 2027), and no step in the pathway rewards a different bachelor's. The field credential in this pathway is the master's, not the bachelor's

Recommendation letters — plan (Nov 24, 2026)

The need: 3 letters per application (PVAMU, MUPEP, PhD); the PhD wants at least two from faculty

Target professors: the concentration-course professors (BUSN301 / BUSN302 / BUSN425) and the BUSN499 Senior Seminar professor first — their subjects match the pathway

Timing: ask at the START of the course, not before enrolling — a letter only counts from a professor who taught you, and APUS sections and instructors post close to the start date

The ask, framed as earning it: state the graduate-school goal, ask what the professor looks for in a student they would recommend and what they would want from you when the time comes (grade, CV, statement draft, deadline)

Become known during the course: discussion posts, questions, office hours, strong papers — in an online class the professor only knows the work you show

Keep them warm after the course: you apply to PVAMU around 2028; send a short update when you apply, with your statement, CV, the program link, and the deadline — 4 to 6 weeks ahead

Target count: cultivate 3 to 4 writers across the remaining terms, so one refusal or one lost contact never empties the bench
