Admissions — TSU MUPEP (master's)

Program: Master of Urban Planning and Environmental Policy, 48 hrs confirmed (24 core + 12 specialization + 12 electives); specializations include Community Development and Housing

Tracks: A non-thesis (120-hr internship + comprehensive exit exam); B thesis (6 thesis credits, no internship/exit exam)

GPA: at least 3.0 in the last 60 undergraduate hours

GRE: REQUIRED of all applicants, scores within 5 years of the entrance term; no minimum score published

Materials: Graduate School application, résumé, 3 letters of reference (preferably professors), official transcripts, statement of purpose 500–750 words; no writing sample

Deadlines (Graduate School table — older; worth one confirmation email): Fall Jul 15, Spring Nov 15, no summer entry

Fee: $75 per the catalog criteria list; $50 domestic / $75 international per a TSU handbook — conflict, flagged

Transfer rule VERIFIED in the 2026-27 catalog: up to 6 hrs with department approval (B or higher, relevant) — and no credit for courses completed for a prior earned degree. A finished PVAMU degree = 0 hrs into MUPEP

Admissions — TSU PhD, Urban Planning and Environmental Policy

Master's required: in an appropriate or related field — it does NOT have to be a planning degree

GPA: 3.2 in master's coursework (department PDF) vs 3.3 cumulative (catalog) — conflict; plan for 3.3

GRE: REQUIRED of all applicants, scores within 5 years of the entrance term; no minimum score published

Materials: Graduate School application + departmental supplement, official transcripts, 3 letters (at least two from faculty), statement (catalog: 500-word minimum; department splits it into four parts, up to ~2,250 words total), résumé, writing sample, personal meeting with the Program Coordinator

Deadline: Feb 15 for fall — the only deadline published

The 24 hrs: official wording — "Students with a Master's degree in planning may apply up to 24 credit hours of Master's coursework towards earning the PhD." It does not name the MUPEP; whether the PVAMU Community Development MS counts as a planning master's is an open question for upep@tsu.edu. The MUPEP locks the 24 in either way

Outside transfer cap: 6 non-dissertation hrs, earned in a doctoral program, grade B or better

Residency: 9 hrs in each of two consecutive semesters before the comprehensive exam, or an approved equivalent (handbook prints one: 9 spring + 6 Summer I + 3 Summer II)

Time limit: 7 calendar years to finish (to 9 with approvals); credits over 7 years old at completion need the Dean's permission; the limit excludes master's coursework

Contacts: upep@tsu.edu · 713.313.6842 · Coordinator Dr. Lalita Sen, Sen_lx@tsu.edu, 713.313.7448

Admissions — the sequence traps

GRE is needed for BOTH TSU steps, never for PVAMU. Scores die at 5 years — a GRE taken for MUPEP entry can expire before PhD entry; plan on a second sitting

The GPA gate that matters most is the MASTER'S GPA (PhD wants 3.2–3.3 in master's coursework) — protect it at PVAMU and at TSU

Letters: 3 at every step; the PhD wants at least two from faculty — build faculty relationships during the master's programs

The PVAMU degree transfers 0 hrs into the MUPEP (verified) — its value is the credential and the GPA, not hours

Two official conflicts to settle by email before applying: PVAMU's real master's deadlines; TSU's real PhD GPA floor (3.2 vs 3.3)
